Overview

Ebook Scroll is a browser extension that provides a smooth, customizable scrolling experience for EPUB and web-based ebooks. Your privacy is important to us. This extension is designed to operate entirely on your device with no data collection, no tracking, and no external data transmission.

Data We Collect

None. Ebook Scroll does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Stored Locally

The extension stores the following configuration data locally on your device using the Chrome storage.local API:

  • Reading preferences (font size, font family, line height, theme)
  • Scroll position and progress through ebooks
  • Recently opened ebook files

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server, third party, or analytics service.

Website Access

The extension runs content scripts on pages where EPUB files are opened solely to:

  • Inject a custom reading interface for EPUB content
  • Apply user-selected reading preferences (theme, font, layout)
  • Provide smooth scrolling behavior

The extension does not read, collect, or transmit any page content, browsing history, user activity, or any other data from these websites.

Permissions

  • storage - Save your reading preferences and progress locally on your device
  • alarms - Trigger scheduled tasks at configured times (if applicable)
  • Host permissions - Inject the reading interface into supported ebook pages

Third-Party Services

The extension loads Google Fonts (Inter, Quicksand) for the reading interface display. This is a CSS-only resource. No user data is shared with Google through this request beyond the standard HTTP headers sent by your browser.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, transfer, or share any user data with third parties for any purpose.

Children's Privacy

This extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
